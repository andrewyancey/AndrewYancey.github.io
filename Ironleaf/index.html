About Our Family-Run Business

Ironleaf Lawncare has proudly served Lexington, Kentucky, and the surrounding community since 1979. What began as a small family-run business has grown through generations while staying true to the same values it was built on: dependable service, quality workmanship, and treating every property like it belongs to a neighbor. Today, Ironleaf offers a full range of lawn and landscape services, including mowing, weedeating, lawn fertilization and weed control, sprinkler system installation, and more.

Whether we're handling routine lawn maintenance or helping improve the long-term health of your yard, our goal is simple: to provide reliable, professional lawn care that keeps your property looking its best season after season.

Our Services

Mowing

Our professional mowing service keeps your lawn looking clean, even, and well-maintained throughout the growing season. We carefully mow your grass to the proper height while maintaining consistent patterns for a crisp, finished appearance. Regular mowing helps promote healthier growth, reduce weeds, and prevent your yard from becoming overgrown. Whether you need routine weekly service or occasional maintenance, we make it easy to keep your property looking sharp without spending your free time behind a mower. Count on us for dependable lawn care and a clean, professionally maintained finish.

weedeating

Our weedeating service gives your property the clean, finished look that mowing alone cannot provide. We carefully trim grass and weeds around fences, trees, flower beds, sidewalks, buildings, and other hard-to-reach areas throughout your yard. Proper trimming helps prevent overgrowth while creating crisp edges and a more polished appearance across the entire property. Perfect as part of routine lawn maintenance, our weedeating service ensures no corners are overlooked. The result is a neat, balanced lawn that looks professionally maintained from the curb to the backyard.

Sprinkler Installation

Our sprinkler installation service provides reliable, convenient watering designed to keep your lawn healthy and evenly hydrated throughout the season. We carefully plan sprinkler placement to provide efficient coverage while reducing dry spots, unnecessary runoff, and wasted water. A properly installed irrigation system makes routine lawn care easier while helping grass, landscaping, and other planted areas receive consistent moisture. Whether you're upgrading your property or installing a system for the first time, we provide a clean, dependable setup designed for long-term performance and hassle-free watering.
